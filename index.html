Reference Manager

A local, offline reference manager. One master library you type yourself, clean .bib files for every project, and your papers read and annotated beside them.

What it does

  • One library, typed by you. Every reference is stored once, with title autocomplete, pasted BibTeX that you check before saving, duplicate checks, and a search with filters.
  • People and venues. Each stored once, with a page of its own. Venues keep the abbreviations that templates choose by tag.
  • Projects. References in nested groups, changes kept in one project or saved to the master, citation keys that never change behind your back, and a check of your LaTeX document.
  • Export. .bib files laid out by templates and kept up to date automatically, plus CSL-JSON, RIS, Typst YAML and formatted reference lists in 79 citation styles.
  • Papers. PDFs added by dropping them in or from watched folders, named by the titles printed in them, read and annotated in the app, with citation cards and a citation graph.
  • And more. Markdown notes that cite your references, sketch boards, a timeline of what you did when, and a browser tab for finding papers on the web.

Your data stays yours

Everything is kept in one folder on your computer: a database, the PDFs and the pictures of marked areas. The app fetches nothing from the internet by itself; web pages load only in the browser tabs you open. The whole library can be saved as one .zip and restored on the same computer or another.

Installing

Windows

Run the installer (64-bit Windows 10 or 11). It is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway. To update, run the newer installer over the old version.

Mac

Open the .dmg and drag Reference Manager into Applications. It runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 11 or later. It is not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so macOS refuses it the first time: try to open it once, then click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security (on macOS 14 or earlier, Control-click the app and choose Open). If macOS says the app "is damaged", run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Reference Manager.app" in Terminal and open it again. To update, quit the app and drag the new version into Applications.

Updating never touches your library, papers or settings.
