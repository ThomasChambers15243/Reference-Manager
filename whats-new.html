0.7.14

Features

  • Import from other reference managers. Export your references from Zotero, Mendeley, JabRef, Paperpile or Overleaf as a .bib file and import it on the Works packages page (or drop it on the window). It becomes a project, as a works package does: the references keep their keys, the PDFs their file fields name come with them, keywords become tags, and JabRef's and Mendeley's groups become the project's groups and categories. Nothing goes into your library until you have checked it. An empty library offers it too.
  • Checking a package's references is easier. A name or a venue that came with the package and is not in your library yet is marked new, with the people or venues of yours it may be ("Smith, J." may be your "Smith, John", "IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory" your "IEEE Transactions on Information Theory"), one click from using yours. A PDF whose printed title differs from the reference's is pointed out.
  • Tips while checking. When you start checking a package's references, a note in the corner says what to look out for. Don't show this again turns it off, and Settings → Works packages turns it back on.
  • Unchecked references are marked. A reference that is not checked yet is marked so in the .bib files written from its project, with a % line above it, and the export says how many there are. In the project it is marked not checked yet.
  • From a paper… has a key. In a note, Ctrl+Shift+P (⌘⇧P on a Mac) opens it.
  • Arrow keys in search lists. The arrow keys and Enter choose from the lists in From a paper…, Add from library, choosing a paper's reference, To a note… and the notes search. In From a paper…, Ctrl+Enter adds what is ticked.
  • Markdown notes take a code editor's keys. Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+Shift+X and Ctrl+E make the selection, or the word at the caret, bold, italic, struck through or code, and take it off again. Ctrl+K makes it a link, and so does a web address pasted over it. Ctrl+Shift+8, Ctrl+Shift+7 and Ctrl+Shift+9 make the lines a list, a numbered list or a task list, and Ctrl+' a quote. Alt+Up and Alt+Down move the lines, with Shift they copy them, and Ctrl+Shift+K deletes them, a numbered list staying numbered in order. A bracket, a quote mark or a mark such as *, _ or ~ typed over a selection goes round it instead of replacing it. Enter on a list item starts the next one, numbered on (an empty one ends the list), and Tab and Shift+Tab move it in and out. A paper's notes written in Markdown take the same keys. Ctrl+Z takes back each change, and a citation put in with @ or Cite…, or what From a paper… brings in, too. The write, read and side-by-side views can be given keys. On a Mac, ⌘ does what Ctrl does.
  • Keys for reading a paper. V, H, U, N, B, E and F choose a tool (select, highlight, underline, note, box, ellipse, freehand), and 1 to 8 a colour of the paper's colour group, as its colour button shows. Alt+Down and Alt+Up go to the next and the previous note, marking it on the page and in the Notes panel without opening it, and O opens it. Back returns to where you were before, once for all the notes gone through. Going to a page, turning to the next or the previous one (two pages side by side turn together), fitting the page and showing the panels have commands too, without keys until you give them some.
  • Keys for moving around the app. They are off unless you turn them on in Settings → Keyboard shortcuts: Alt+L for the library, Alt+P for papers, Alt+N for notes and one for each other page, Ctrl+, for Settings, Alt+B to narrow or widen the sidebar and Ctrl+Shift+F for the page's search box. While they are on, the sidebar names them. On a Mac they are ⌥ and ⌘ keys, and the ⌥ ones wait while you type, since ⌥ types letters there.
  • Every shortcut can be changed. In Settings → Keyboard shortcuts the commands are grouped by where they work (writing notes, reading a paper, tabs and windows, anywhere, moving around the app, sketch boards, the timeline, web pages) and found by what they do or by their key. Click a key and press the new one. A command can have several keys, or none, and a key another command has is refused, with that command's name. Buttons and hints show the keys chosen. Duplicating a tab, moving it to a new window and closing the other tabs can be given keys too, and so can New note and New reference, which then work on any page. Alt+Left and Alt+Right no longer go back and forward while you type in a text box, as on a Mac already.
  • A list of the page's keys. Ctrl+/ (⌘/ on a Mac) lists the keys of the page you are on, from the same list as Settings, with a link there to change them.
  • Keys in web pages. The app's keys work while a web page in a tab has the keyboard, as chosen in Settings: every key for tabs and windows, and the keys for moving around when they are on. Ctrl+L goes to the address bar from the page and from the bar above it.
  • A choice of search site. Settings → Web links chooses the site a browser tab opens on and searches, and that a citation is looked up on: Google Scholar, OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar.
  • The window's title shows the version: Reference Manager 0.7.14.
  • The new icon everywhere. The installer shows the app's icon, and makes Windows show the new icon on the taskbar and the desktop: it kept the old one in its cache.

Bug fixes

  • One unreadable setting no longer resets the rest. When one setting could not be read (one a newer version saved another way, say), every setting went back to its default. Now only that one does.
  • Spaces where PDF lines end. Text taken from a PDF ran the words at the end of one line and the start of the next together ("end ofthe line") in a highlight's quote, and so in what was copied or brought into a note. Lines are now joined with a space, and a word a hyphen breaks at a line's end is joined again. Copying from a PDF with Ctrl+C gives the same text.

0.7.12

Features

  • Works packages. A project in one file, published by a lab, a research group or a person. It holds references in groups, their categories and tags, notes and, if its maker chooses, the PDFs with their highlights. A new Works packages page, between Templates and Settings, imports them, exports any project as one, and shows how to write one by hand or with an AI.
  • A package opens as a project. It says first what it holds and what it leaves out. Its references stay out of your library until you bring them in, each checked in the reference form first, and they then take the package's categories and tags. A reference your library already has is used as it is; one it has by its title can be swapped for yours. Their keys in the project never change.
  • Its PDFs come too. A package's PDFs go into Papers, linked to their references. Its highlights and notes are added only to PDFs that are new to you, so they never mix with yours. Removing a package takes back what is still only its own.
  • Import by dropping. Drop a works package (.zip or .json) on the window to import it.
  • The library format changes (to 9). Updating from an earlier version saves a copy of your library in backups\ first.

Bug fixes

  • None in this version.

0.7.11

Features

  • Updates on a Mac. As on Windows, a few seconds after it starts, the app says when a new version is out, and Update and restart installs it. The new version takes the old one's place in Applications and opens. Copies older than 0.7.11 are updated by hand once.
  • Updates come from the website. On Windows and on a Mac, the app asks the website when it starts (Settings → Updates can turn that off), downloads the new version and checks it against the size and checksum the website gives before installing it. Versions built on the same computer are still found in their folder.
  • Settings has a contents list on the left. Click a section to go to it; the one you're reading is marked as you scroll. The list scrolls by itself when the window is short, and hides when it is narrow.
  • arXiv as a venue. It is a kind of venue, Eprint archive, which a reference's eprint type (archivePrefix in BibTeX) draws on. A preprint shows arXiv as its venue in the library, venue:arxiv finds it, and arXiv has a page like any journal. Eprint types already typed in become venues of that kind.
  • Categories and tags for references. They have their own, in a sidebar beside the library table, with Not organised yet.
  • References, papers, people and venues are organised the same way. Drag one, or every one ticked, onto a category or tag. Drag a category onto another to put it inside, onto the line above one to move it there, or out to the top level. Drag a tag up or down the list to reorder it. Add to category and Tag work on the ticked ones everywhere, and Remove from category or Remove the tag takes them out of the one shown. These actions float in a bar at the foot of the list, so ticking the first one no longer moves the list.
  • The library format changes (to 8). Updating from an earlier version saves a copy of your library in backups\ first.

Bug fixes

  • Install from a file… takes the website's downloads: installers named as the website names them (Reference-Manager_0.7.11_x64-setup.exe) and, on a Mac, a .dmg.

0.7.10

Features

  • None in this version.

Bug fixes

  • Search: phrases with a colon. A quoted phrase with a colon in it ("BERT: Pre-training…") is searched for, instead of being read as a filter and finding nothing.
  • Search: backwards ranges. A range that ends before it starts (year:2023..2019, date:2019-03) now says so, instead of quietly finding nothing.
  • Formatted references: question marks. No full stop or comma after a title ending in ? or ! ("need? Journal", not "need?. Journal"), and "Why? A study" instead of "Why?: A study".
  • DOIs printed once. In formatted references, CSL-JSON and RIS, a DOI typed as a link (https://doi.org/10.…) is no longer printed twice.
  • Formatted references print names as typed ("d'Alembert", not "d' Alembert"; "van Gogh, V.", not "Gogh, V. van").
  • Accents copied from PDFs. A name copied from a PDF with its accent stored apart no longer creates a second copy of the same person. .bib files get the accents joined up, which pdfLaTeX needs.
  • The people list counts each reference once, however many projects changed its author list.
  • Notes saved as Markdown. A line of = under a comment no longer makes it a heading, an indented line no longer turns into code, the title is escaped, and a picture whose note ends in \ still shows.
  • A project's timeline export. A note on a paper since linked to a reference outside the project stays with the project's reference.
  • Colour groups: Duplicate with long names. It works for names close to the 60-character limit.
  • Pasting long BibTeX is much faster.

0.7.9

Features

  • None in this version.

Bug fixes

  • A form in a background tab no longer freezes the window. Links inside the form (a person, a venue, a note, a citing paper) open their tab behind it, and the tab shortcuts wait while a form or dialog is open.
  • Mac tab shortcuts. On a Mac the tab shortcuts are ⌘T, ⌘W and ⌘1–9 only, because Ctrl+T swaps two letters in a text box there.
  • Keys: Set key… while files are written. A key typed with Set key… while linked files were being written is no longer overwritten.
  • Keys: one group's file. Copying or saving one group's file from Export & sync records (and with locked keys pins) only the keys in that file, and only when the copy worked.
  • Keys: Use the new keys. It no longer marks a project that was never written out as written.
  • Templates: deleted tags stay deleted. A Templates page opened before an abbreviation tag or a kind of venue was deleted no longer saves it back.
  • Templates: unsaved edits kept. The sidebar's Templates link no longer throws them away.
  • .bib files:
    • A month stored on its own uses the template's month style (jun, June).
    • In BibLaTeX, the subtitle is protected like the title.
    • Field order and omit lists naming year or school also work for BibLaTeX's date and institution.
    • The [key] token writes Müller as Muller, not Mller.
  • A pinned key gets no "its typed key is used" warning.
  • A project's formatted references keep your order.
  • Export & sync opens on the project's own file, so the warnings button shows that file's warnings.
  • Check document: every tick counts. Its "Select them in the project" shows every reference.
  • Add to project counts right. It counts only the references that weren't in the project already.

0.7.8

Features

  • None in this version.

Bug fixes

  • Closing a tab or window keeps what you were typing: notes on people and venues, a paper's details, notes on a page, text on a board.
  • Ctrl+Enter keeps a half-typed name. In the reference form it adds a name you're still typing instead of dropping it, and no longer picks a suggestion as well.
  • Reference form:
    • Asks before Reset, Push to master, or opening a PDF or project would drop your other changes.
    • A new reference's form starts empty.
    • A second pasted BibTeX text shows its own form.
    • Saving to one project only no longer turns an old date into the project's own.
  • Early dates kept as typed. A year before Christ (-0044) is left as typed; years below 1000 keep four digits (0800-03).
  • Paper viewer:
    • A reference opened after starting "Type its reference" no longer gets linked to the paper.
    • Page notes and the Notes panel no longer undo each other's changes, and quick changes in Details no longer undo each other.
    • Find works on pages you've scrolled away from.
    • A reopened paper shows the right page number.
    • Numbered citations lead to the right reference.
    • Pinch zoom follows your fingers instead of jumping.
  • Papers dragged onto a category are put in it.
  • The citation graph keeps its view when you come back to its tab.
  • Projects: hidden references left alone. The selection count and Remove ignore references hidden by a group or filter.
  • People and venues:
    • A merge starts afresh for each pair, and Cancel keeps the selection.
    • A right click no longer opens a link.
    • Typing in one abbreviation survives adding another.
  • Settings keeps the key pattern you're editing when another setting is saved.
  • Templates:
    • Built-in templates can no longer be reordered by dragging.
    • Duplicate asks about unsaved changes.
    • Emptying a text chip no longer deletes it.
  • Boards: groups and reopening. A group only goes into a larger one (no more refused saves), and a board reopens where you left it.
  • Notes: saving and folders. An undo during a save no longer leaves the note unsaved, and a project's notes keep their own open folders.
  • Timeline: shading stays in place. It stays with the days, months and years as you move along.

0.7.7

Features

  • None in this version.

Bug fixes

  • Security: web pages kept out of the app. A web page in a browser tab could load the app in a frame and, on Windows, use the app's commands. Both are now refused.
  • Security: no scripts in links. A javascript: link in formatted references no longer runs. Links there and in the timeline export open as web links instead of taking over the window.
  • Web pages can no longer press the app's keys.
  • Windows:
    • A tab dragged to another window no longer opens in every window.
    • Windows fit small or scaled screens and open on the screen you dropped a tab on.
  • Mac: Quit saves your typing. On a Mac, Quit (⌘Q) saves what you were typing, as closing a window does, and Option+←/→ moves by a word again.
  • Pre-update copies are kept by date, so the newest is no longer deleted.
  • Browser tabs:
    • Get the PDF stops waiting after half a minute.
    • A PDF downloaded for a deleted reference still arrives.
    • Add this PDF works again after a failed download.
    • A tab remembers its reference.
  • Footnotes in notes lead to their note instead of an error.

0.7.6

Features

  • None in this version.

Bug fixes

  • Backups can't overwrite the library. A backup saved over the library's own file could delete the library. That's refused now.
  • Partial restores reported properly. A restore that partly worked was reported as failed, and could lose the copy of the previous library.
  • Writing linked files:
    • No longer overwrites an edit made outside the app at the same moment.
    • Keeps the file's permissions on a Mac.
    • No longer replaces a shortcut (symlink) with a plain file.
  • Watched folders:
    • PDFs in a folder that couldn't be read are no longer forgotten.
    • Half-downloaded files wait until they're complete.
    • A failed import is tried again.
  • Adding a PDF again brings back its stored file if it went missing.
  • Search in papers. Letters like ı, ø, ł, ß and æ now match, and results are no longer cut off for big libraries.
  • Relinking a paper to another reference updates its citation matches.
  • Notes:
    • Citations written as @{key} are linked.
    • Saving notes as files gives a usable name to one Windows reserves (like Aux).
  • Deleted things stay gone. A new project no longer inherits a deleted project's timeline. A deleted abbreviation tag or kind of venue is taken out of templates.
  • Merging tidies project copies. Merging people or venues no longer leaves project copies identical to the library's.

0.7.5

Features

  • None in this version.

Bug fixes

  • .bib files:
    • A part of a book with its own title is an @incollection in BibTeX, instead of losing the book's title.
    • Initials like J.A. stay J.A. (not J.).
    • A month kept beside a year-only date is no longer dropped.

    • Line breaks inside a value follow the template's line endings.
  • Renaming keys in .tex files stops safely. It stops if an \input can't be read, instead of skipping that file.

0.7.3

Features

  • Colour groups filter the page. The colours chosen in the notes panel also hide the other highlights on the page. While the panel is closed, a bar says what's shown, with Show all.
  • cola keys: no year after +N (SharkeyCBLW+24).
  • README.md and features.md are up to date.

Bug fixes

  • Ticks no longer follow you between projects. Going from one project to another in the same tab kept the first project's ticks, and Remove could then take references out of it.
  • Text typed just before closing a window is kept (it was lost).
  • Board settings no longer bring back an old board. Changing a board's settings could bring back, and save, an older version of the board.
  • Linked file formats. Changing a linked file's format wrote the new format into the old file.
  • Templates in use can't be deleted. Deleting a template that a project's extra file used quietly changed that file. It's now refused.
  • Set key… no longer re-keys another reference.
  • LaTeX accents in names. Names written like Mu\~{n}oz were split apart.
  • Short family names. Pasting a name with a short family name (Li, Wu) could create a duplicate person.
  • Smaller fixes in search, tags, the timeline and notes.

0.7.1

Features

  • Papers are named by the title printed in them instead of their file name.
  • The library as a .zip: save everything (database, PDFs, pictures) and restore it, from Settings → Your data.
  • cola keys write more initials: up to four more authors, then +N.

Bug fixes

  • Citation cards find the reference lists of many more papers.
  • Hover cards show reliably, and clicking a citation goes to its reference.
  • Going back no longer leaves a blue box over the page.
  • Cancel and Stop ask first. Cancel on a reference and Stop in a pasted-BibTeX review ask before throwing changes away.
  • Ctrl+Shift+W closes the window, not the tab, and Light or Dark reaches every open window.

Known issues

  • Maths in titles. LaTeX maths in a title ($\alpha$-Synuclein) shows without the symbol in the library table and in formatted references.
  • One board in two windows. If the same board is edited in two windows at the same moment, the last save wins for an item changed in both.
